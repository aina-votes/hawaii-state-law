## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§16-3] Election services,
> provisions, and charges. The chief election officer may make voting
> systems and election services available to state agencies and private entities
> pursuant to rules adopted in accordance with chapter 91. Reasonable fees may
> be charged for the provision of such systems or services. [L 1993, c 304, §3]
