## Statute text (verbatim)

> §14-30 Lists of persons voted for and
> number of votes, certification, transmission to president of the senate.
> The electors shall separately list the persons voted for as president and as
> vice president, respectively, and the number of votes for each, which lists
> they shall sign, certify, seal, and transmit by mail, one copy to the seat of
> the government of the United States, directed to the president of the senate of
> the United States, and make such other disposition of the lists as required by
> law. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2]
