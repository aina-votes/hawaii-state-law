## Statute text (verbatim)

> §16-43 Ballot handling. In every case
> where the ballots are handled by election officials or election employees for
> disposition upon completion of the tabulation, they shall be handled in the
> presence of not less than two officials assigned in accordance with section
> 16-45. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1975, c 36, §5(7); am L 2019, c 136, §35]
