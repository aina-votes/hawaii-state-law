## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-352] False name contributions
> prohibited. (a) No person shall make a contribution to any candidate, candidate
> committee, or noncandidate committee in any name other than that of the person
> who owns the money, property, or service.
>
> (b) All contributions made in the name of a
> person other than the owner of the money, property, or service shall escheat to
> the Hawaii election campaign fund. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
