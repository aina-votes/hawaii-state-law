## Statute text (verbatim)

> §14-31 Compensation and mileage of
> electors, amounts. Each presidential elector shall receive $50 for the
> elector's services, plus the reasonable cost of transportation from the
> elector's voting residence in the State to the state capital and return. Their
> accounts shall be certified to by the chief election officer and audited by the
> state comptroller, and shall be payable out of the general fund. [L 1970, c 26,
> pt of §2; gen ch 1985]
