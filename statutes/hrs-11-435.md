## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-435] Report and recommendation.
> In January of each year, the commission shall submit to the legislature:
>
> (1) A study and recommendations of reasonable
> campaign expenditure and contribution limits and the factors which may be
> relevant in their establishment; and
>
> (2) A report concerning the status of the Hawaii
> election [campaign] fund. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
