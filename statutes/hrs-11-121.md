## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-121] Ballot images. A voter shall
> not be prohibited from distributing or sharing an electronic or digital image
> of the voter's own marked ballot via social media or other means regardless of
> how the voter acquired the image; provided that this section shall not be a
> defense for any election offenses under chapter 19 or related offenses under
> the Penal Code. [L 2016, c 81, §1]
