## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-157 In case of tie. In case of the
> failure of an election by reason of the equality of vote between two or more
> candidates, the tie shall be decided by the chief election officer or clerk in
> the case of county elections by lot. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; gen ch 1985; am L
