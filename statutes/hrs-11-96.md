## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-96 Records prima facie evidence.
> Every record made pursuant to law by a board of registration of voters, or the
> precinct officials, shall be a prima facie evidence of the facts therein set
> forth, and shall be received as such in any court or tribunal in which the same
> is offered in evidence. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1973, c 217, §1(cc)]
