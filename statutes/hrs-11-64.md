## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-64 Names of party officers to be filed.
> All parties shall submit to the chief election officer and the respective
> county clerks not later than 4:30 p.m. on the one
> hundred seventieth day prior to the next primary, a list of names and
> addresses of officers of the central committee and of the respective county
> committees. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1973, c 217, §1(r); am L 1983, c 34,
> §6; am L 2022, c 4, §3]
