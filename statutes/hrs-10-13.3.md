## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§10-13.3] Interim revenue.
> Notwithstanding the definition of revenue contained in this chapter and the
> provisions of section 10-13.5, and notwithstanding any claimed invalidity of
> Act 304, Session Laws of Hawaii 1990, the income and proceeds from the pro rata
> portion of the public land trust under article XII, section 6 of the state
> constitution for expenditure by the office of Hawaiian affairs for the
> betterment of the conditions of native Hawaiians for each of fiscal year
> 1997-1998 and fiscal year 1998-1999 shall be $15,100,000. [L 1997, c 329, §2]
