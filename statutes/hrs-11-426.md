## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-426 Candidate exceeds voluntary
> expenditure limit. A candidate who files the affidavit agreeing to limit
> expenditures and who exceeds the expenditure limit for that election shall:
>
> (1) Notify all opponents, the office of elections,
> and the commission by telephone and writing on the day the expenditure limit is
> exceeded;
>
> (2) Pay the balance of the full filing fee; and
>
> (3) Provide reasonable notice to all contributors
> within thirty days of exceeding the limit that the expenditure limit was
> exceeded. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2; am L 2014, c 140, §1]
