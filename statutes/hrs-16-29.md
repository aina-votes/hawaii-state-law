## Statute text (verbatim)

> §16-29 Tally sheets. The tally sheets
> used in counting the ballots cast shall be marked and handled in a secure
> fashion prescribed in rules and regulations promulgated by the chief election
> officer in accordance with chapter 91. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2]
