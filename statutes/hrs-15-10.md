## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15-10 Counting of absentee ballots.
> If the requirements in section 15-9 are met, the return and ballot envelopes
> may be opened and the ballot counted as prescribed by law for the voting system
> in use. [L 1975, c 36, pt of §3; am L 1976, c 106, §4(5); am L 1980, c 248,
