## Statute text (verbatim)

> §91-13 Consultation by officials of agency.
> No official of an agency who renders a decision in a contested case shall
> consult any person on any issue of fact except upon notice and opportunity for
> all parties to participate, save to the extent required for the disposition of
> ex parte matters authorized by law. [L 1961, c 103, §13; Supp, §6C-13; HRS
> §91-13]
