## Statute text (verbatim)

> §16-27 Number of blank and questionable
> ballots; record of. In addition to the count of the valid ballots, the
> voter service center officials shall, as to each separate official ballot, also
> determine and record the number of totally blank ballots and the number of
> questionable ballots. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1973, c 217, §6(e); am L
> 2021, c 213, §42]
