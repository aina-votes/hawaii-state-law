## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-42 Compensation. Members of the
> boards of registration shall be compensated pursuant to a schedule established
> by the chief election officer. The schedule shall be contained in rules
> adopted pursuant to chapter 91. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1979, c 133, §1;
