## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-372] Reporting loan; written
> loan agreement. (a) Every loan shall be reported as provided in section
> 11-333.
>
> (b) Every loan in excess of $100 shall be
> documented as provided in section 11-333.
>
> (c) A loan shall be treated as a contribution,
> subject to all relevant provisions of this part, if the loan is not reported or
> documented as provided in section 11-333. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
