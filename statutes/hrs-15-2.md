## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15-2 Who may vote by absentee ballot.
> Any person registered to vote may cast an absentee ballot in any election,
> including an election conducted by mail, in the manner provided in this chapter
> and rules adopted by the chief election officer. [L 1975, c 36, pt of §3; am L
