## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-6 Petitions; withdrawal of
> signatures. Wherever in this chapter the signatures of registered voters
> are required on a petition, any voter who, after signing a petition, seeks to
> withdraw the voter's signature may do so by providing notice in writing to the
> chief election officer any time before the filing of the petition. The notice
> shall include the voter's name, residence address, the month and date portions
> of the voter's date of birth, the voter's signature, and a statement that the
> voter wishes to remove the voter's signature from the petition; provided that
> the written notice shall not require the voter's social security number or any
> portion thereof and the year portion of the voter's date of birth. Upon
> receipt of that notice containing the information required by this section, the
> chief election officer shall notify the group or individual to whom the
> petition was issued and the signature of the individual shall not be counted.
> [L 1993, c 304, §1; am L 2022, c 164, §1]
