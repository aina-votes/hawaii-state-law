## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-345] Donors; consent; notice. (a)
> A nonprofit organization subject to this chapter and operating as a
> noncandidate committee may use a donation for
> electioneering communications, independent expenditures, or contributions only
> upon receipt of written consent from the donor to use the donation for
> electioneering communications, independent expenditures, or contributions.
>
> (b) A nonprofit
> organization subject to this chapter and operating as a noncandidate committee
> shall provide written notice to donors that the donor's name and address may be
> reported pursuant to this chapter in a public document if the donor provides
> written consent to use the donation for electioneering communications,
> independent expenditures, or contributions.
>
> (c) If the donor fails to provide written
> consent that the donation may be used for electioneering communications,
> independent expenditures, or contributions, the nonprofit organization shall,
> within thirty days of receipt of the donation, transmit to the donor a written
> confirmation by the nonprofit organization's highest ranking official that the
> donation will not be used for electioneering communications, independent
> expenditures, or contributions; and the name and address of the donor will not
> be reported by the nonprofit organization pursuant to this chapter.
>
> (d) Only a nonprofit organization subject to
> this chapter and operating as a noncandidate committee shall be subject to the
> reporting requirements of this section. [L 2022, c 169, §2]
