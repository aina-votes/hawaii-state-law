## Statute text (verbatim)

> §50-4 Qualifications of commission members.
> Each charter commission member shall be a registered voter and resident of the
> county for at least three years prior to the member's appointment. Elected
> officials of the state or county governments shall not be eligible for
> appointment. [L 1963, c 73, pt of §2; Supp, §143A-4; HRS §50-4; gen ch 1985]
