## Statute text (verbatim)

> §14-26 Assembly of electors at state
> capital; time. The electors chosen shall assemble at the state capital on
> the first Tuesday after the second Wednesday in December next following their
> election, at two o'clock in the afternoon. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 2024,
