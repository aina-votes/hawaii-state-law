## Statute text (verbatim)

> §25-4 Penalty for violation and false
> evidence. Any person who, having been summoned under section 25-3 to give
> testimony or to produce any books, records, files, papers, maps and documents,
> wilfully makes default, or who, having appeared, refuses to answer any
> questions or wilfully gives false evidence shall be fined not more than $1,000,
> or imprisoned not more than twelve months, or both. [L 1969, c 79, §4]
