## Statute text (verbatim)

> §25-1 Reapportionment commission. (a)
> A reapportionment commission shall be constituted before May 1 of each
> reapportionment year, and the members shall be appointed and certified to hold
> office until a general election is held under a reapportionment plan of the
> commission, or of a court of competent jurisdiction, or a new commission is
> constituted under article IV, section 2 of the state constitution, whichever
> event shall occur first.
>
> (b) The reapportionment commission shall be
> placed within the office of elections for administrative purposes only.
>
> (c) In each regular session of the legislature
> that immediately precedes a reapportionment year, the chief election officer
> shall request an appropriation that is separate from the office of elections'
> operating budget and sufficient to enable the commission to carry out its
> duties, to be effective in the year in which the reapportionment commission is
> constituted. [L 1969, c 79, §1; am L 1979, c 51, §2; am L 1982, c 249, §2; am L
> 1992, c 320, §2; am L 2012, c 223, §1]
