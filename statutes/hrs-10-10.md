## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§10-10] Administrator; appointment,
> tenure, removal. The board by a majority vote, shall appoint an
> administrator who shall serve without regard to the provisions of chapter 76
> for a term to be determined by the board. The board, by a two-thirds vote of
> all members to which it is entitled, may remove the administrator for cause at
> any time. [L 1979, c 196, pt of §2; am L 2000, c 253, §150]
