## Statute text (verbatim)

> §50-12 Tenure of commission. The terms
> of office of the members of the charter commission shall expire on the day
> after the election at which the proposed charter is submitted to the qualified
> electors of the county for approval, unless earlier terminated as provided in
> section 50-6. [L 1963, c 73, pt of §2; Supp, §143A-13; HRS §50-12]
