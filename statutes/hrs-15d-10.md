## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15D-10 Receipt
> of voted ballot. A valid
> military-overseas ballot shall be counted if it is received by the close of the
> voter service center on the day of the election and meets the requirements for
> absentee ballots pursuant to section 15-9. [L 2012, c 226, pt of §1; am L 2021,
> c 213, §39]
