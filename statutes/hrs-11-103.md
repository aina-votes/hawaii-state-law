## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-103] Public notice of mailing.
> Public notice of the date or dates on which the initial ballot packages are to
> be mailed shall be given by the clerks before the ballot packages are made
> available to voters. [L 2019, c 136, pt of §2]
