## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-343] Reporting deadline. When
> any reporting deadline falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or holiday designated in
> section 8-1, the reporting deadline shall be the next succeeding day that is
> not a Saturday, Sunday, or holiday. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
