## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-43 Powers; procedures. (a) Each
> board of registration is given all of the powers and authority for the
> summoning and examining of witnesses and the maintenance of order, including
> the power to punish for contempt and award witness fees in accordance with
> section 621-7, by law given to circuit courts.
>
> (b) Every member of the board of registration
> may administer oaths in all cases in which oaths are by law authorized.
>
> (c) The procedures for challenges and appeals
> under sections 11-25 and 11-26 and this part shall be exempt from the
> provisions of chapter 91 regarding contested case hearings, but shall be
> administered according to rules adopted by the chief election officer. [L 1970,
> c 26, pt of §2; am L 1990, c 45, §8]
