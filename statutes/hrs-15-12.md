## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15-12 Receipt and disposition of late
> absentee ballots. For any election all return envelopes containing
> absentee ballots received by the clerk after the deadline for receipt stated in
> section 15-9 shall be kept unopened and disposed of pursuant to section 11-154.
> [L 1975, c 36, pt of §3; am L 1980, c 248, §1(h)]
