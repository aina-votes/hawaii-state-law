## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§91-4.4] Form of publication. The
> revisor of statutes shall determine the form in which the Hawaii administrative
> rules index and its supplements shall be published. Either or both of the
> publications may be issued in units, in bound or loose-leaf form, separately or
> in combination, at the same or different times, as the revisor considers most
> economical and best adapted to make the index available to interested persons
> and the public. [L 1979, c 216, pt of §2]
