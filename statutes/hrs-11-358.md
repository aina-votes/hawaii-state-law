## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-358] Contributions to
> noncandidate committees; limits. No person shall make contributions to a
> noncandidate committee in an aggregate amount greater than $1,000 in an
> election. This section shall not apply to ballot issue committees. [L 2010, c
> 211, pt of §2]
