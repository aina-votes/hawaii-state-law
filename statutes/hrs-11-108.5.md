## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-108.5] Validity
> of cast mail-in ballot where voter later becomes ineligible. The mail-in ballot of any voter who was eligible
> to vote at the time the ballot was cast shall not be deemed invalid solely
> because the voter became ineligible to vote, including by death of the voter,
> after casting the ballot. For the purposes of this section, "cast"
> means that the voter has:
>
> (1) Returned the return identification envelope
> containing the optional secrecy envelope or secrecy sleeve with the marked
> ballot in any manner permitted pursuant to section 11-104(c); or
>
> (2) Completed voting in person at a voter service
> center. [L 2023, c 113, §2]
