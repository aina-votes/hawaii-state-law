## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-111 Official and facsimile ballots.
> Ballots issued by the chief election officer in state elections and by the
> clerk in county elections are official ballots. In elections using the paper
> ballot and electronic voting systems, the chief election officer or clerk in
> the case of county elections shall have printed informational posters
> containing facsimile ballots that depict the official ballots to be used in the
> election. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1973, c 217, §1(ee); am L 1975, c 36,
> §1(4); am L 1980, c 264, §1(f); am L 2019, c 136, §15]
