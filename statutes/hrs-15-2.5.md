## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15-2.5 Voting by mail in precinct affected
> by natural disasters. (a) If the chief election officer and clerk of a
> county affected as a result of a natural disaster determine that the opening of
> a designated voter service center will adversely affect the health and safety
> of voters or voter service center officials, the chief election officer and
> county clerk, by written order, may require the registered voters of any
> precinct to vote by mail as provided in part VIIA of chapter 11.
>
> (b) Within thirty days after the issuance of
> such an order, the chief election officer and county clerk shall notify all
> registered voters in the affected precinct of the issuance of the order.
>
> (c) The chief election officer shall adopt
> rules pursuant to chapter 91 to implement this section. [L 1996, c 215, §1; am
> L 2019, c 136, §27; am L 2021, c 213, §34]
