## Statute text (verbatim)

> §14-23 Time for election, number to be
> chosen. In each presidential election year there shall be elected at
> large, at the general election, by the voters of the State, as many electors
> and alternates of president and vice president of the United States as the
> State is then entitled to elect, in the manner provided under section 11-113.
> The electors and the alternates must be registered voters of the State. The
> election shall be conducted and the results thereof determined in conformity
> with the laws governing general elections except as otherwise provided. [L
> 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1977, c 189, §3]
