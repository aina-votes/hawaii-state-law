## Statute text (verbatim)

> §50-13 Compensation of commission members.
> Members of the charter commission shall receive as compensation for their
> services the sum of $1,000 each, and shall be reimbursed by the county for
> their necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their duties. [L 1963,
> c 73, pt of §2; Supp, §143A-14; HRS §50-13]
