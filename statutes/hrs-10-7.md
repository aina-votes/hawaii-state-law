## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§10-7] Board of trustees. The office
> of Hawaiian affairs shall be governed by a board to be officially known as the
> board of trustees, office of Hawaiian affairs. Members of the board shall be
> elected in accordance with chapter 13D, with reference to sections 11-15,
> 11-25, 12-5, 12-6, and vacancies shall be filled in accordance with section
> 17-7. [L 1979, c 196, pt of §2]
