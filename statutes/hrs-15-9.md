## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15-9 Return, receipt, processing, and
> treatment of absentee ballots. An absentee
> ballot shall be returned, received, processed, and treated in the same manner
> as a return identification envelope in an election by mail as provided by part
> VIIA of chapter 11. [L 1975, c 36, pt of §3; am L 1976, c 106, §4(4); am
> L 1979, c 133, §8; am L 1980, c 248, §1(f); am L 1983, c 34, §22; am L 1985, c
