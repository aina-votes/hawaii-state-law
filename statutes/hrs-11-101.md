## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-101 Elections eligible to be conducted
> by mail. Beginning with the 2020 primary election, all elections shall be
> conducted by mail in accordance with this title. A voter in an election conducted by mail shall not be precluded
> from voting by absentee ballot under chapter 15 or 15D, if the voter complies
> with the applicable requirements. [L 2019, c 136, pt of §2; am L 2021, c
> 213, §17]
