## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-383] Exceptions.
> Notwithstanding sections 11-381 and 11-382:
>
> (1) A party may support more than one candidate; and
>
> (2) A candidate for the office of governor or
> lieutenant governor may support a co-candidate in the general election. [L
> 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
