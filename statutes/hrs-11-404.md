## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-404] Initial determination by
> the commission. The commission shall promptly determine, without regard to
> chapter 91, to:
>
> (1) Summarily dismiss the complaint;
>
> (2) Investigate further;
>
> (3) Make a preliminary determination; or
>
> (4) Refer the complaint to an appropriate prosecuting
> attorney for prosecution under section 11-411. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
