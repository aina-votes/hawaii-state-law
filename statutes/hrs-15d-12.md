## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§15D-12] Federal
> write-in absentee ballot. A covered voter may use a federal write-in
> absentee ballot to vote for all offices and ballot measures in an election
> described in section 15D-3. [L 2012, c 226, pt of §1]
