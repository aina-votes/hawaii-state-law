## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§15D-13] Confirmation of receipt
> of application and voted ballot. The chief election officer, in
> coordination with local election officials, shall implement an electronic
> free-access system by which a covered voter may determine by telephone,
> electronic mail, or Internet whether:
>
> (1) The voter's federal postcard application or other
> registration or military-overseas ballot application has been received and
> accepted; and
>
> (2) The voter's military-overseas ballot has been
> received. [L 2012, c 226, pt of §1]
