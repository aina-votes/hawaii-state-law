## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-421] Hawaii election campaign
> fund; creation. (a) The Hawaii election campaign fund is created as a
> trust fund within the state treasury.
>
> (b) The fund shall consist of:
>
> (1) All moneys collected from persons who have
> designated a portion of their income tax liability to the fund as provided in
> section 235-102.5(a);
>
> (2) Any general fund appropriations; and
>
> (3) Other moneys collected pursuant to this part.
>
> (c) Moneys in the fund shall be paid to
> candidates by the comptroller as prescribed in section 11-431 and may be used
> for the commission's operating expenses, including staff salaries and fringe
> benefits. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
