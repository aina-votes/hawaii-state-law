## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§15D-5] Overseas voter's registration address. In registering to vote, an overseas voter who is eligible
> to vote in this State shall use and be assigned to the voting district of the person's
> residence, or the last place of residence prior to leaving this State, or, in
> the case of a voter described by paragraph (4) of the definition of
> "covered voter" under section 15D-2, the address of the last place of
> residence in this State of the parent or legal guardian of the voter. If that
> address is no longer a recognized residential address, the voter shall be
> assigned a district for voting purposes. [L 2012, c 226, pt of §1]
