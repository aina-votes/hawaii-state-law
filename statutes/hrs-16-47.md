## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§16-47] Preparation of absentee ballots.
> Counting center employees in the presence of at least two official observers
> shall prepare absentee ballots for counting by automatic tabulating equipment
> in a manner that shall accurately reflect the votes cast by the absentee
> voters. [L 1980, c 264, §5(b)]
