## Statute text (verbatim)

> §16-28 Declaration of results. When
> the voter service center officials have ascertained the number of votes given
> for each candidate they shall make public declaration of the whole number of
> votes cast, the names of the persons voted for, and the number of votes for
> each person. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1973, c 217, §6(f); am L 2021, c
> 213, §43]
