## Statute text (verbatim)

> §16-2 Voting system requirements. All
> voting systems adopted under this chapter by the chief election officer or the
> legislature shall satisfy the following requirements:
>
> (1) It shall secure to the voter secrecy in the act
> of voting;
>
> (2) It shall provide for voting for all candidates of
> as many political parties as may make nominations, nonpartisans, and for or
> against as many questions as are submitted;
>
> (3) It shall correctly register or record and
> accurately count all votes cast for any and all persons, and for or against any
> and all questions. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2]
