## Statute text (verbatim)

> §12-2 Primary held when; candidates only
> those nominated. The primary shall be held on the second Saturday of August
> in every even numbered year.
>
> No person shall be a candidate for any general
> or special general election unless the person has been nominated in the
> immediately preceding primary or special primary. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L
> 1973, c 217, §2(a); am L 1975, c 36, §2(1); am L 1976, c 106, §2(1); am L 1979,
