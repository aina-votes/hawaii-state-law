## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-15.5] Duties of all state agencies;
> voter registration. Each state agency that deals with the public shall
> make available to each member of the public eighteen years of age or older an
> application in the form of an affidavit for voter registration pursuant to
> section 11-15. The application shall be available by mail or in person
> depending on the manner in which the agency's services are requested by the
> person. The form of the application may be identical to that described and
> found in public telephone directories. [L 1993, c 100, §1]
