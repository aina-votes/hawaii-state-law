## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§10-14.6] Legislative review.
> The legislature shall consider the board's proposed program and financial plan;
> evaluate alternatives to the board's recommendations; and appropriate any
> general fund portion of the budget and any matching special fund
> appropriations. [L 1990, c 221, pt of §2]
