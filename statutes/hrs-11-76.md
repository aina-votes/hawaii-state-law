## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-76 Compensation. Election
> officials under the supervision and control of the office of elections on
> election day shall be compensated pursuant to a schedule established by the
> chief election officer. The schedule shall be contained in rules adopted
> pursuant to chapter 91. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1973, c 217, §1(y); am L
> 1974, c 19, §1; am L 1977, c 151, §2; am L 1982, c 145, §1; am L 1989, c 155,
