## Statute text (verbatim)

> §50-11 Charter amendment and revision.
> Every charter established under this chapter shall provide means by which the
> charter may be amended or revised. The provisions for amendment and revision
> must provide for approval of all amendments and revisions by referendum to the
> electors of the county. The amendment or revision shall be considered ratified
> if a majority of the electors voting on the amendment or revision cast their
> ballots in favor of adoption. [L 1963, c 73, pt of §2; am L 1965, c 65, §1(5);
> Supp, §143A-12; HRS §50-11]
