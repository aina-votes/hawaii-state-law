## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-99 Members of Congress, applicability
> of election laws. The nomination and election of a senator or
> representative to Congress shall be in conformity to the laws applicable to the
> election of members of the state legislature except as expressly otherwise
> provided or where in conflict with federal law. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2]
