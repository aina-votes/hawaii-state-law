## Statute text (verbatim)

> §25-8 Records, reports. The commission
> shall keep a written record of its meetings and hearings and shall submit a
> written report to the legislature twenty days prior to the regular session next
> convening. [L 1969, c 79, §8; am L 1992, c 320, §4]
