## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-402] Filing of complaint.
> (a) A person alleging violations of this part shall file a complaint with the
> commission.
>
> (b) A complaint initiated by the commission
> shall be in writing and signed by the executive director.
>
> (c) A complaint by a person other than the
> executive director shall be in writing, signed by the person filing the
> complaint, and notarized. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
