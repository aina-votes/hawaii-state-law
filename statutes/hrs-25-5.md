## Statute text (verbatim)

> §25-5 Compensation. Each of the
> members of the reapportionment commission selected and certified shall, for the
> period the member holds the member's office, receive compensation of $50 per
> meeting but not to exceed $1,000 per month and shall be allowed actual and
> necessary expenses incurred in the performance of the member's duties.
> Payments for compensation and expenses shall be paid by warrants signed by the
> comptroller upon vouchers properly endorsed by the chairperson of the
> commission. The members of the commission shall be exempt from the provisions
> of chapter 76. [L 1969, c 79, §5; am L 1973, c 217, §9(a); gen ch 1985, 1993;
> am L 2000, c 253, §150]
