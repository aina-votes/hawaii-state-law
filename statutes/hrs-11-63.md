## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-63 Party rules, amendments to be filed.
> All parties must file their rules with the chief election officer not later
> than 4:30 p.m. on the one hundred seventieth
> day prior to the next primary. All amendments shall be filed with the chief
> election officer not later than 4:30 p.m. on the thirtieth day after their
> adoption. The rules and amendments shall be duly certified to by an authorized
> officer of the party and upon filing, the rules and amendments thereto shall be
> a public record. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1973, c 217, §1(q); am L 1983, c
> 34, §5; am L 1986, c 323, §3; am L 2022, c 4, §2]
