## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-362] Contributions limited from
> nonresident persons. (a) Contributions from all persons who are not
> residents of the State at the time the contributions are made shall not exceed thirty
> per cent of the total contributions received by a candidate or candidate
> committee for each election period.
>
> (b) This section shall not be applicable to
> contributions from the candidate's immediate family. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
