## Statute text (verbatim)

> §12-21 Official party ballots. The
> primary or special primary ballot shall be clearly designated as such. The
> names of the candidates of each party qualifying under section 11-61 or 11-62
> and of nonpartisan candidates may be printed on separate ballots, or on a
> single ballot. The name of each party and the nonpartisan designation shall be
> distinctly printed and sufficiently separate from each other. The names of all
> candidates shall be printed on the ballot as provided in section 11-115. When
> the names of all candidates of the same party for the same office exceed the
> maximum number of voting positions on a single side of a ballot card, the
> excess names may be arranged and listed on both sides of the ballot card and
> additional ballot cards if necessary. When separate ballots for each party are
> not used, the order in which parties appear on the ballot, including
> nonpartisan, shall be determined by lot.
>
> The chief election officer or the county clerk,
> in the case of county elections, shall approve printed samples or proofs of the
> respective party ballots as to uniformity of size, weight, shape, and thickness
> prior to final printing of the official ballots. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L
> 1973, c 217, §2(f); am L 1979, c 139, §7; am L 1981, c 214, §1; am L 1987, c
> 232, §2]
