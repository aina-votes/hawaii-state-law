## Statute text (verbatim)

> §50-1 Applicability. All counties
> which do not have a charter and all counties which have a charter may create a
> charter commission as provided herein. No provision of this chapter shall be
> held to alter or restrict any provision of any existing charter, except as
> hereinafter specifically provided. [L 1963, c 73, pt of §2; am L 1965, c 65,
> §1(1); Supp, §143A-1; HRS §50-1]
