## Statute text (verbatim)

> §11-140 Spoiled ballots. In elections
> using the paper ballot and electronic voting systems, if a voter spoils a
> ballot, the voter may obtain another upon returning the spoiled one. Before
> returning the spoiled ballot, the voter shall conform to the procedure
> promulgated by the chief election officer to retain the secrecy of the vote. [L
> 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1973, c 217, §1(uu); am L 1975, c 36, §1(11); am L
> 1980, c 264, §1(o); am L 1981, c 100, §1(3)]
