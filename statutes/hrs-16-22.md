## Statute text (verbatim)

> §16-22 Marking. The method of marking
> a paper ballot shall be prescribed by the chief election officer by rules and
> regulations promulgated in accordance with chapter 91. The chief election
> officer shall prescribe a uniform method of marking the ballots in all precincts
> in a county and for absentee voting by paper ballot. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2;
> gen ch 1985]
