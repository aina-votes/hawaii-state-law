## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15-13.5 Validity
> of cast absentee ballot where voter later becomes ineligible. The absentee ballot of any voter who was eligible to vote
> at the time the ballot was cast shall not be deemed invalid solely because the
> voter became ineligible to vote, including by death of the voter, after casting the ballot. For the purposes of this
> section, "cast" means that the voter has:
>
> (1) Deposited the absentee ballot in the mail for
> ballots mailed in accordance with section 15-9;
>
> (2) Delivered the absentee
> ballot to the appropriate county clerk or polling place in accordance with
> section 15-9; or
>
> (3) Completed voting in
> person at an absentee polling place. [L 2019, c 10, §1; am L 2021, c 213, §38;
> am L 2022, c 109, §1; am L 2023, c 113, §3]
