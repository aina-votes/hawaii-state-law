## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§11-382] Prohibited uses of
> campaign funds. Campaign funds shall not be used:
>
> (1) To support the campaigns of candidates other than
> the candidate with which they are directly associated;
>
> (2) To campaign against any other candidate not
> directly opposing the candidate with which they are directly associated; or
>
> (3) For personal expenses. [L 2010, c 211, pt of §2]
