## Statute text (verbatim)

> §50-7 Powers of the commission. The
> charter commission shall hold public hearings and sponsor public forums and in
> general provide for the widest possible public information and discussion
> respecting the purpose and progress of its work. It shall receive the
> assistance of any officer or employee of the county without extra compensation
> as it may request to carry out its functions. It may, with the approval of the
> legislative body of the county, appoint staff members and consultants as it
> deems necessary. [L 1963, c 73, pt of §2; Supp, §143A-7; HRS §50-7]
