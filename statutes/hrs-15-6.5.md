## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15-6.5 Absentee postage. The
> mailed distribution and return of absentee ballots shall be at no cost to the
> voter. The State and counties shall share in the cost of all postage
> associated with the distribution and return of absentee ballots pursuant to
> sections 11-182 and 11-183, if the costs are not covered by the federal
> government. [L 1993, c 304, §2; am L 2019, c 136, §29]
