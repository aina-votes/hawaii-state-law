## Statute text (verbatim)

> §12-2.5 Nomination papers; when available.
> Nomination papers shall be made available from the first working day of February
> in every even-numbered year; provided that in the case of a special primary or
> special election, nomination papers shall be made available at least ten days prior
> to the close of filing. [L 1979, c 133, §7; am L 1990, c 35, §6; am L 2021, c
> 14, §§4, 9(2)]
