## Statute text (verbatim)

> [§10-33] Powers herein, additional to other
> powers. The powers conferred by this part shall be in addition and
> supplemental to the powers conferred by any other general, special, or local
> law. Insofar as this part is inconsistent with any other general, special, or
> local law this part shall be controlling. [L 1994, c 283, pt of §2(2)]
