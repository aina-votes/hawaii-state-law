## Statute text (verbatim)

> §16-11 Definitions. "Protective
> counter" means an apparatus built into the voting machine which cannot be
> reset, which records the total movement of the operating lever.
>
> "Voting machine system" means the
> method of electrically, mechanically, or electronically recording and counting
> votes upon being cast. [L 1970, c 26, pt of §2; am L 1975, c 36, §5(2)]
