## Statute text (verbatim)

> §15D-10.5 Validity
> of cast military-overseas ballot where covered voter later becomes ineligible.
> The military-overseas ballot of any covered voter who was eligible to vote at
> the time the ballot was cast in accordance with this chapter shall not be
> deemed invalid solely because the covered voter became ineligible to vote,
> including by death of the voter, after casting the ballot. [L 2019, c 10, §2; am L 2023, c 113, §4]
