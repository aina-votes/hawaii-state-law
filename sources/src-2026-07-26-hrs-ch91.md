# HRS Chapter 91 — Administrative Procedure (frontier ingest)

The first chapter pulled in by the **citation frontier** rather than the Office of Elections
list: before this ingest, ch. 91 was the most-cited body of law outside the corpus (31
operative cites), and the CSC rule-text ingest ([[src-2026-07-25-csc-har-rules]]) raised the
pressure to 148 wikilinks to [[hrs-91-2|§91-2]] alone — every procedural rule in
[[har-3-161]] rests on it. This is Hawaii's Administrative Procedure Act: the law governing
how agencies (including the Campaign Spending Commission) adopt rules, hold contested-case
hearings, and get reviewed by courts.
