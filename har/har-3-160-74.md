## Rule text (verbatim)

> (a) After a complaint is filed with the commission, the executive director may enter into a conciliation agreement with the person(s) against whom the complaint is filed, to resolve the complaint. (b) A conciliation agreement shall be approved by the commission at a meeting conducted under chapter 92, Hawaii Revised Statutes.
