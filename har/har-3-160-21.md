## Rule text (verbatim)

> (a) Any person must register as a noncandidate committee if the person receives contributions or makes expenditures, the aggregate amount of which is more than $1,000, in a two-year election period. (b) The noncandidate committee's reports must include information about: (1) Its expenditures (e.g., contributions to Hawaii state and local candidates); and (2) Its contributions received by the noncandidate committee. (c) The noncandidate committee must segregate contributions and expenditures to Hawaii committees in a separate bank account or by a ledger account in the noncandidate committee's main account.
