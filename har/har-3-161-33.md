## Rule text (verbatim)

> Before the hearing, each respondent may file and serve a written response stating briefly therein facts, circumstances, laws, rules, or reasons in defense and shall further specifically admit or deny the allegations of the petition upon: (1) Each petitioner or complainant, (2) The commission, and (3) Hearings officer, if applicable.
