## Rule text (verbatim)

> The commission may determine without regard to chapter 91, Hawaii Revised Statutes, to summarily dismiss a complaint if: (1) The complaint fails to comply with the requirements of section 3-160-70; (2) The complaint fails to provide statements that were based upon sufficient knowledge or information to form a belief that the statements are true; (3) The complaint fails to provide sufficient statements to support a claim that a law or rule may have been violated; or ( 4) The complaint clearly lacks merit.
