## Rule text (verbatim)

> Argument shall be heard either before the commission or a hearings officer duly designated. All parties shall be afforded full opportunity to present argument on all issues involved. The argument shall be at the time and place set forth in the notice of argument but at that time and place may be continued from day to day and adjourned to a later day or to a different place without notice other than the announcem]ft thereof at the hearing.
