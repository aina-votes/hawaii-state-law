## Rule text (verbatim)

> Unless the context specifically indicates otherwise, as used in this chapter: "Excess contributions" means contributions received by a candidate or committee in excess of any contribution limits in sections 11-357, 11-358, 11-359(b), and 11-360, Hawaii Revised Statutes. "Matching payment period" means: (1) For a primary election, from January 1 of the year of a general election through the day of the primary election; and (2) For a general election, from January 1 of the year of a general election through the day of the general election. "Sundry item" includes clothing, bumper stickers, pins, buttons, and similar small items upon which the notice required by section 11-391(a), Hawaii Revised Statutes, cannot be conveniently printed.
