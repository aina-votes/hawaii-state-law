# HAR §3-160-11 — Commission chairperson and vice

## Rule text (verbatim)

> (a) A chairperson shall be elected by a majority of all the members to which the commission is entitled who shall serve for a term of one year or until a successor is elected. (b) A vice-chairperson, who shall call and chair meetings in the incapacity or absence of the chairperson, shall be elected by a majority of all the members to which the commission is entitled, and shall serve for a term of one year or until a successor is elected.
