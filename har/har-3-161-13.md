## Rule text (verbatim)

> Unless otherwise provided, the commission or hearings officer may extend the time within which any action shall be taken at the request of any party. The commission or hearings officer, in the commission's or hearing officer's sole discretion, may require that the extension be stipulated to by all parties to the proceeding or that the request be by motion for good cause shown as to why the extension should be granted.
