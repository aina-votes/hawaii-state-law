## Rule text (verbatim)

> (a) "Limited partnership, limited liability partnership, or limited liability limited partnership", as used in this section, means business entities that are recognized as such under the laws of the state in which they are established. (b) A contribution from a limited partnership, limited liability partnership, or limited liability limited partnership shall be treated as a contribution from a partnership.
