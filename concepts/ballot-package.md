# Ballot package

The statutory term for what an all-mail voter receives. Defined by contents, not by name.

## What must be in it

[[hrs-11-102|HRS §11-102(a)]] requires four items:

1. An official ballot
2. A return identification envelope **with postage prepaid**
3. A secrecy envelope or secrecy sleeve
4. Instructions

## What must be on the outside

§11-102(c): the exterior of the envelope must carry instructions for obtaining language
translation services **in Hawaiian** and, at minimum, the five most-used foreign languages among
limited-English-proficient persons statewide.

## Operational notes

- **Postage is prepaid.** Voters never need a stamp. Worth stating affirmatively in GOTV copy —
  "do I need a stamp" is a real friction point.
- **Mailed non-forwardable.** See [[mail-ballot-registration-cutoff]] for why this matters.
- The State's published target is that voters receive the package ~18 days out; for 2026 the OoE
  says ballots arrive by **July 21** (primary) and **October 16** (general). See [[deadlines]].
